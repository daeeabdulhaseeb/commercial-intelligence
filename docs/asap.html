Backlog → next-month invoice, with conversion rate

Each month's closing backlog (pale) paired with the invoice it produced the next month (dark). Line = invoice ÷ prior backlog — the monthly drain rate.

Drain-rate trend — throughput health

Drain rate = what % of the backlog you invoice each month (3-month average of invoice ÷ backlog). Historically ~10%. Flat = steady delivery · falling = backlog converting slower (clogging, delivery slowing) · rising = burning the backlog down faster than refilling it.

Team balance —

Each team's backlog inflow (award) vs outflow (invoice/drain). Balanced = winning ≈ delivering, backlog stable · Building = winning faster than delivering (backlog piling up) · Draining = billing down backlog faster than refilling.
